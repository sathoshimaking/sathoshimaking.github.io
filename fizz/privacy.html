Fizz Cascade

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version. Fizz Cascade has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. Your progress, lives, coins, daily streak and settings are stored on your device and are never sent to the developer.

The app does not ask you to identify yourself, and the developer never receives your name, email address, or payment details. Two third parties are involved, and only in narrow ways: Apple processes purchases, and Google serves the game's ads. Both are described below.

1. Who is responsible

Fizz Cascade is made and published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy, or about data the app handles, go to emre.yildirimm1910@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your device's local app storage. It stays on the device, is not transmitted to the developer, and is not shared with anyone:

  • Which levels you have completed
  • The level you are currently playing, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Your lives and when the next one arrives
  • Your coin balance and Undos, and a short record of how the balance changed
  • Your daily-puzzle state: streak, best streak, which days you played, and the result of each day's first attempt (for the share line)
  • Which free hints you have used
  • Settings: sound, haptics, tap to preview, and colour palette
  • Simple ad counters: how many ad lives you have used today, and how recently an ad was shown, so the game can keep ads infrequent
  • A record of the purchases you made, so that you never lose what you bought

None of this identifies you. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple's device backup may include it; that copy is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Deleting the app removes all of it from the device.

3. What the developer collects

Nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no server that the app talks to. The developer cannot see how you play, when you play, or whether you play.

When you choose to share your daily result, the app hands a short line of text to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is your choice. The app itself sends nothing.

4. Advertising

Fizz Cascade shows two kinds of ads, both delivered by Google AdMob and both limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G).

Rewarded ads (always your choice)

A rewarded ad plays only after you tap a button that offers something in exchange: an extra life when you are out of lives, or a second chance on a level you just lost (from level 21 on). It never starts on its own, and no part of the game is locked behind one.

Ads between levels

From level 21 on, an ad may appear between levels after a win, when you move on to the next level. These are kept infrequent: at most one every three wins and at least three minutes apart, never during a puzzle, never after a loss, never on the first win of a session, never on the daily puzzle, and never in the first 20 levels. Buying Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack turns them off for good; the optional rewarded ads stay available.

Who serves them

When an ad is requested, Google receives information needed to select and deliver that ad and to detect fraud. This typically includes:

  • A device identifier
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location is inferred
  • Device and app information — model, operating system version, app version
  • Interaction with the ad — that it was shown, watched, or closed
  • Diagnostic information about ad delivery

This data is handled by Google under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads are not personalised

The app requests non-personalised ads only. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so you will not see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no advertising profile is built about you through this app. Non-personalised ads are chosen from the general context — such as the app itself and coarse location — rather than from your past behaviour.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested: either the first time you tap a button that offers an ad, or at a quiet moment between levels once you have finished level 19 — never in the middle of a puzzle. Your choice is remembered, and you can change it later in the app's Settings, under Privacy choices.

5. Purchases

The app offers four optional, one-off in-app purchases: Remove Ads, the Supporter Pack (a sixth life slot, an extra colour palette, and no ads between levels), +5 Lives, and Unlimited Lives for 4 hours. They are processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. What the app receives is Apple's confirmation that a purchase completed, which it uses to unlock what you bought.

Remove Ads and the Supporter Pack can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing, and can be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases. Unlimited Lives for 4 hours applies to the device it was bought on and is not restored. There are no subscriptions. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

Fizz Cascade is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains third-party advertising, limited to general-audience content and reflected in its App Store age rating.

7. Your choices

  • Skip the optional ads. Every rewarded ad is your choice, and no part of the game is locked behind one.
  • Turn off ads between levels with Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack.
  • Change your ad consent at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Adjust Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Delete the app. This erases everything the game stored on your device. Nothing is retained elsewhere, because nothing was sent elsewhere.

8. Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request a copy of and nothing to delete. Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data held by Google in connection with ad delivery is retained under Google's policies, linked above.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes — particularly any change to what is collected — will be reflected here before the app version making the change is released.

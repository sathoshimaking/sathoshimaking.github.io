Fizz Cascade

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Türkçe

The short version. Fizz Cascade has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. Your progress, lives, coins, daily streak and settings are stored on your device, and the saved game is never sent to the developer.

The app does not ask you to identify yourself, and the developer never receives your name, email address, or payment details. On iOS, two third parties are involved, and only in narrow ways: Apple processes purchases, and Google serves the game's ads. Both are described below. On Android, Google Play processes purchases instead of Apple, and from version 1.2 the game also uses Google Analytics and, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, TikTok to measure how the game is played and which ads bring new players. Section 6 covers everything that differs on Android.

1. Who is responsible

Fizz Cascade is made and published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy, or about data the app handles, go to sonictradingteknoloji@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your device's local app storage. It stays on the device, is not transmitted to the developer, and is not shared with anyone:

  • Which levels you have completed
  • The level you are currently playing, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Your lives and when the next one arrives
  • Your coin balance and Undos, and a short record of how the balance changed
  • Your daily-puzzle state: streak, best streak, which days you played, and the result of each day's first attempt (for the share line)
  • Which free hints you have used
  • Settings: sound, haptics, tap to preview, and colour palette
  • Simple ad counters: how many ad lives you have used today, and how recently an ad was shown, so the game can keep ads infrequent
  • A record of the purchases you made, so that you never lose what you bought

None of this identifies you. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple's device backup may include it; that copy is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. (Android backup is covered in section 6.) Deleting the app removes all of it from the device. On Android, the game events in section 6 report some of the same moments, such as a level finished or a daily streak, as they happen; the saved game itself is not sent.

3. What the developer collects

On iOS: nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no server that the app talks to. The developer cannot see how you play, when you play, or whether you play.

On Android, from version 1.2, the game sends game events to Google Analytics and, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, to TikTok, so the developer can see how the game is played and which ads bring players. There is still no account, no sign-in, no crash-reporting service and no server of the developer's own. Section 6, under Measurement on Android, says exactly what is sent, to whom, and what you can do about it.

When you choose to share your daily result, the app hands a short line of text to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is your choice. The app itself sends nothing.

4. Advertising

Fizz Cascade shows two kinds of ads, both delivered by Google AdMob and both limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G).

Rewarded ads (always your choice)

A rewarded ad plays only after you tap a button that offers something in exchange: an extra life when you are out of lives, or a second chance on a level you just lost (from level 21 on). It never starts on its own, and no part of the game is locked behind one.

Ads between levels

From level 21 on, an ad may appear between levels after a win, when you move on to the next level. These are kept infrequent: at most one every three wins and at least three minutes apart, never during a puzzle, never after a loss, never on the first win of a session, never on the daily puzzle, and never in the first 20 levels. Buying Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack turns them off for good; the optional rewarded ads stay available.

Who serves them

When an ad is requested, Google receives information needed to select and deliver that ad and to detect fraud. This typically includes:

  • A device identifier
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location is inferred
  • Device and app information — model, operating system version, app version
  • Interaction with the ad — that it was shown, watched, or closed
  • Diagnostic information about ad delivery

This data is handled by Google under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads are not personalised

The app requests non-personalised ads only. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so you will not see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no advertising profile is built about you through the game's ads. Non-personalised ads are chosen from the general context — such as the app itself and coarse location — rather than from your past behaviour.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested: either the first time you tap a button that offers an ad, or at a quiet moment between levels once you have finished level 19 — never in the middle of a puzzle. Your choice is remembered, and you can change it later in the app's Settings, under Privacy choices.

5. Purchases

The app offers four optional, one-off in-app purchases: Remove Ads, the Supporter Pack (a sixth life slot, an extra colour palette, and no ads between levels), +5 Lives, and Unlimited Lives for 4 hours. They are processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. What the app receives is Apple's confirmation that a purchase completed, which it uses to unlock what you bought.

Remove Ads and the Supporter Pack can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing, and can be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases. Unlimited Lives for 4 hours applies to the device it was bought on and is not restored. There are no subscriptions. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Fizz Cascade on Android

The Android version of Fizz Cascade, on Google Play, works the same way as the iOS version described above, with these differences. The last one, Measurement on Android, applies from version 1.2.

Purchases through Google Play

On Android, the same four purchases are processed entirely by Google Play, not Apple. The developer never sees your card details, billing address, or Google Account. What the app receives is Google Play's confirmation that a purchase completed, which it checks and uses to unlock what you bought; it keeps a record of that confirmation on your device so that you never lose what you bought.

Some payment methods take time to clear. While a payment is pending, nothing is unlocked and the app says so; the purchase arrives as soon as Google Play confirms the payment.

Remove Ads and the Supporter Pack belong to the Google Account that bought them, and come back on any Android device signed in to that account with Restore Purchases (in the Store and in Settings). Google Play does not share in-app purchases with family members, so there is no Family Sharing on Android. Unlimited Lives for 4 hours applies to the device it was bought on and is not restored. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Google's backup service may keep a copy of your progress, coins, daily streak, settings, and purchase record, so that they come back when you set up a new phone. That copy is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one. A few items are deliberately kept out of the backup and never leave the device: the lives timer's clock check, the ad counters, the consent progress, and Unlimited Lives for 4 hours. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Ads on Android

On Android, ads are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. Everything in section 4 applies unchanged: non-personalised ads only, rated G, the same limits on ads between levels, the same consent form in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and Privacy choices in the app's Settings.

When an ad is requested, Google receives the information listed in section 4. On Android, the device identifiers are the Android advertising ID and the app set ID. Because only non-personalised ads are requested, Google uses them for things like frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention, not to personalise ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on most phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads); the game's ads keep working without it.

Sharing

When you share your daily result on Android, the app hands a short line of text to Android's share sheet. The app itself sends nothing.

Measurement on Android

From version 1.2, the Android app uses two measurement tools: Google Analytics for Firebase and TikTok's measurement SDK. Neither is part of the iOS app.

Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google (Google LLC and its affiliate Google Ireland Limited), receives:

  • App opens, sessions and time spent in the app (collected automatically)
  • Game events: levels started and finished, taps used, hints, undos, continues, rewarded ads watched, daily streaks, the tutorial finished, and the Store opened
  • Purchases: the product, its price and currency, from Google Play
  • The ad revenue of each ad shown
  • The app-instance ID (a random ID Firebase creates for this installation) and the Android advertising ID
  • An approximate location, inferred from your IP address
  • The device model and operating system version
  • Where the app was installed from (the Google Play install referrer)

Why: to understand how the game is played, and to measure which Google ad campaigns bring players. Google Analytics does not receive your name, email address or any account ID, and its data is not used to personalise ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, nothing is sent to Google Analytics until you consent in Google's consent form (the same form as for ads). You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app's Settings, under Privacy choices; from then on nothing more is sent.

TikTok (TikTok Pte. Ltd. and its affiliates) is used outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only. It receives:

  • That the game was installed and opened
  • That the tutorial was finished
  • That a level milestone was reached
  • Purchases: the product, its value and currency
  • Ad revenue
  • The Android advertising ID, and an ID TikTok creates for this installation
  • Device and network information, such as the device model, operating system, IP address, and where the app was installed from

Why: to measure which TikTok ads brought the install. TikTok is never started in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, whatever you choose in the consent form. See TikTok's Privacy Policy.

How to limit it (all regions): reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on most phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads); the other data above is still sent. Uninstalling the app stops all of it.

Requests: for questions or requests about the data described here, write to sonictradingteknoloji@gmail.com.

7. Children

Fizz Cascade is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains third-party advertising, limited to general-audience content and reflected in its App Store age rating and its Google Play content rating.

8. Your choices

  • Skip the optional ads. Every rewarded ad is your choice, and no part of the game is locked behind one.
  • Turn off ads between levels with Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack.
  • Change your consent for ads (and, on Android, for Google Analytics) at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies).
  • Limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or on Android reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Adjust Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Delete the app. This erases everything the game stored on your device. On iOS nothing is retained elsewhere, because nothing was sent elsewhere. On Android, what Google Analytics and TikTok already received (section 6) is kept under their policies.

9. Data retention and deletion

On iOS, the developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request a copy of and nothing to delete. Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data held by Google in connection with ad delivery is retained under Google's policies, linked above. On Android, the measurement data described in section 6 is kept by Google Analytics and by TikTok under their own policies; for questions or requests about it, write to sonictradingteknoloji@gmail.com.

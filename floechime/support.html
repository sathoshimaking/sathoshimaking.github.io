Floe Chime support

Something not working, or just a question? Start here. If the answer isn't here, write to sonictradingteknoloji@gmail.com.

Restore my purchases

Bought Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack and it's gone, say on a new phone? Open the game on a phone signed in to the same Google account that bought it, then tap Restore Purchases. You'll find it in two places: in the Store (from the Menu), and in Settings.

The game answers with one line:

  • Purchases restored: they're back.
  • Purchases already active: nothing was missing.
  • Nothing to restore (Remove Ads and Supporter Pack only): Google Play doesn't list either of those for the account on this phone. Check you're signed in to the account you bought with.
  • Couldn't reach Google Play. Try again later.: check your connection and try again.

Only Remove Ads and the Supporter Pack can be restored. +5 Lives and the Unlimited passes (4 hours or 1 day) are used up as you play, so they don't come back, and a pass works only on the phone where you bought it.

My payment is pending

Some payment methods (for example some cards, carrier billing or cash top-ups) take a while to clear. While that happens the game says Payment pending. You'll get it once it's paid. and nothing is unlocked yet. You don't need to do anything: the game checks with Google Play when it opens and when you come back to it, and a card on the Menu tells you when the purchase arrives. If it's still missing after Google Play shows the payment as complete, tap Restore Purchases, then write to us.

Refunds and order history are handled by Google Play: see Google's help on refunds.

Lives, and what to do when you're out

You start with 5 lives and get one back every 20 minutes. The Supporter Pack adds a sixth life slot. A life is spent when you run out of fish on a level, or when you tap Restart or Leave with fewer fish than the level started with (levels 11 to 40 start with 2 fish, the others with 3), or after you used a continue. Undo takes the board back but not the fish you lost. The tutorial levels and the Daily never cost a life.

At zero lives, the Out of lives window offers:

  • Wait: it shows Next life in and counts down.
  • Watch an ad: +1 life, up to 5 a day. It resets at midnight.
  • +5 Lives, Unlimited 4h or Unlimited 1 day: paid options (the price is shown by Google Play on the row; the passes are for this phone only).
  • Store: opens the Store with everything above.

Remove the ads

Open the Store and choose Remove Ads (no interstitial ads; the optional rewarded ads stay) or the Supporter Pack (6th life, no ads, supports the game). A row that says Owned is already yours. There are no ads at all in the first 20 levels apart from the optional Watch an ad for a life.

Change my privacy choices

  • Everyone: Settings → Share usage and crash data. Turn it off to stop usage statistics and crash reports (and delete any crash reports waiting to be sent).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Settings → Privacy Choices reopens Google's consent form so you can change your answer. The row appears only where Google's consent tool applies. If it says Privacy choices couldn't open. Try again in a moment., check your connection and try again.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings, usually Settings → Privacy → Ads.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

What to put in a bug report

A few details help us find the problem quickly:

  • Your phone's make and model, and its Android version (Settings → About phone).
  • The game's version (on its Google Play page, under About this app).
  • Which level (its number, like Level 23) or Daily # number you were on.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can.
  • For a purchase problem: the Google Play order number from your receipt email (it starts with GPA.), and which product it was.
  • If you use TalkBack, large text or Remove animations, tell us. It sometimes matters.

Please don't send passwords, card numbers or other private details.

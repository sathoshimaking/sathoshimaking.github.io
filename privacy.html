Ghost Access: Hack Puzzle Privacy Policy · Last updated: 19 October 2026 The short version. Ghost Access: Hack Puzzle has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. Your progress, lives, coins, daily streak and settings are stored on your device and are never sent anywhere. The app does not ask you to identify yourself, and the developer never receives your name, email address, or payment details. Two third parties are involved, and only in narrow ways: Apple processes purchases, and Google serves the optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Both are described below. 1. Who is responsible Ghost Access: Hack Puzzle is made and published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy, or about data the app handles, go to: • emre.yildirimm1910@gmail.com 2. What the app stores on your device All of the following is written to your device's local app storage. It stays on the device, is not transmitted to the developer, and is not shared with anyone: • Which levels you have completed, and your results on them • Your current lives and when the next one regenerates • Your coin balance and the record of how it changed • Your daily-challenge state: streak, best streak, and which days you played • Hints used • Settings — sound on or off, screen effect on or off None of this identifies you. If your device is set to back up to iCloud, Apple's own device backup may include it; that copy is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Deleting the app removes all of it from the device. 3. What the developer collects Nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no server that the app talks to. The developer cannot see how you play, when you play, or whether you play. 4. Advertising Ghost Access: Hack Puzzle shows rewarded ads only. A rewarded ad never starts on its own: it plays only after you tap a button that offers something in exchange — an extra life, or a second chance at a puzzle you just lost. There are no ads between levels, no ads on launch, and no ads that interrupt a puzzle in progress. You can play the entire game without watching one. Who serves them Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch one, Google receives information needed to select and deliver that ad and to detect fraud. This typically includes: • A device advertising identifier • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location is inferred • Device and app information — model, operating system version, app version • Interaction with the ad — that it was shown, watched, or closed • Diagnostic information about ad delivery This data is handled by Google under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ads are not personalised The app requests non-personalised ads. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so you will not see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no advertising profile is built about you through this app. Non-personalised ads are chosen from the general context — such as the app itself and coarse location — rather than from your past behaviour. Consent If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad is requested — that is, the first time you tap a button that offers an ad, not when you open the app. If you never tap one, you are never asked. If you answered, your choice is remembered, and you can change it later from the app's settings. 5. Purchases The app offers optional in-app purchases. These are processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. What the app receives is a receipt confirming that a purchase completed, which it uses to unlock what you bought. There are no subscriptions. Every purchase is one-off. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy. 6. Children Ghost Access: Hack Puzzle is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains third-party advertising, which is reflected in its App Store age rating. 7. Your choices • Don't watch ads. Every ad in the game is optional, and no part of the game is locked behind one. • Reset your device advertising identifier, or limit ad tracking, in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. • Adjust Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com. • Delete the app. This erases everything the game stored on your device. Nothing is retained elsewhere, because nothing was sent elsewhere. 8. Data retention and deletion The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request a copy of and nothing to delete. Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data held by Google in connection with ad delivery is retained under Google's policies, linked above. 9. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes — particularly any change to what is collected — will be reflected here before the app version making the change is released. 10.
